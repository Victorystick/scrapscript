Apps

Web apps in scrapscript, in the Elm architecture: a model, messages that update it, and a view of it.

Scrap

App


      
An app is web model msg, whose interface is web.scrap: init takes flags, here #null, and update a message; both give the next model and the commands to carry out, such as HTTP requests. view and subscriptions say what the page shows and what the app listens to. Scraps that it refers to by $source: hash come from the yard, and a step that runs longer than a second fails. The address bar keeps what you ran, so you can share it.
